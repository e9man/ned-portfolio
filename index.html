Mission

Map a route to No Evidence of Diseasefor every person.

The space of health

Every body is a point in a space with thousands of dimensions.

Each thing we can measure, such as tumor size, immune cells, blood chemistry, sleep or genes, is one dimension of this space. NED is the origin, point zero, and disease is the distance from it.

Every action moves the point. Some bring it closer to zero, and some push it away. The map shows which is which, and each measurement shows where the person really is. The mission is to find the route back to the origin for every person.

How it works

Every person makes the map better

01

The map

A century of cancer research, including the newest discoveries, holds great potential that has not yet turned into cures.

Missing until now

The research sits in unstructured text, spread over fields that rarely meet.

What changed

With super intelligence, a small team can now understand research from every field. Doctors, researchers and engineers form a multidisciplinary group, building a living knowledge map of all research.

02

Clinical exploration

The map shows promising directions to test with patients, and every result goes back to update the map.

Missing until now

Feedback from patients came slowly, usually from a scan every few months.

What changed

Measurement tools are becoming more and more accessible and affordable. The measurement platform runs N-of-1 programs and fast clinical trials, with high-frequency measurements. A dedicated clinical team of special-operations veterans runs each person's program every day, from tests to treatments.
